I work on the conformal bootstrap: constraining critical phenomena using only symmetry, unitarity and consistency, and studying the mathematics these constraints lead to. I am also interested in complex systems, AI, and how intelligence emerges. 我的研究围绕共形自举展开：仅依靠对称性、幺正性与自洽性来约束临界现象，并研究由此引出的数学问题。我也关注复杂系统、AI 技术以及智能的涌现。

During graduate study at Wuhan University I worked on lattice QCD calculations of charm-meson decays. 在武汉大学读研期间，我从事粲介子衰变的格点 QCD 计算。

Research interests研究兴趣

Conformal bootstrap and its mathematics共形自举及其衍生的数学问题

Numerical and analytic bootstrap of conformal field theories, including thermal CFTs and deconfined quantum criticality, and the mathematical structures behind these constraints.共形场论的数值与解析自举，包括有限温共形场论与退禁闭量子临界点，以及这些约束背后的数学结构。

Complex systems and AI复杂系统与 AI 技术

Collective behavior in many-body systems, and AI methods as tools for physics research.多体系统中的集体行为，以及将 AI 方法用作物理研究的工具。

Emergence of intelligence智能的涌现

How intelligent behavior arises from simple components, viewed through the lens of statistical physics.从统计物理的视角理解智能行为如何从简单单元中涌现。
